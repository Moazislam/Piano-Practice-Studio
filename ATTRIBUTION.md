# Piano sample attribution

The embedded piano recordings are from the [Salamander Grand Piano V2 sample library](https://github.com/Tonejs/audio/tree/master/salamander), a Yamaha C5 sampled by **Alexander Holm**. The library states that its samples are licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported (CC BY 3.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).

This project includes 21 original MP3 note recordings and pitches them to play the adjacent notes. The recordings are credited here as required by the license. The webpage’s original code is provided separately from the sample recordings; this attribution does not claim that the sample library was created for this project.
